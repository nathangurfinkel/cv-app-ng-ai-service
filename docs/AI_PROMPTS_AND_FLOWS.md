# AI Prompts and Flows Map

This document maps every AI-backed flow in the service to its entry point, source location, and prompt behavior. Use it for audits and when changing prompts.

**Where prompts live:**

- **app/services/ai_service.py** – Most flows: extract, tailor, evaluate, rephrase, inject_keyword, elaborate, improve_from_feedback, recommend_template, and the shared helper `summarize_job_requirements`. Generic helpers `generate_completion` and `stream_completion` (no fixed prompt) are also here.
- **app/routes/roast_routes.py** – Roast: inline system and user prompts, then `llm.chat_completion`.
- **app/routes/interview_routes.py** – Interview start, answer (stream), and analyze: prompts built in the route, then `ai_service.generate_completion` or `ai_service.stream_completion`.

## Notes

- **summarize_job_requirements** is used only by flows that need compressed JD context: rephrase (non-default instruction types), inject_keyword, elaborate_with_keyword, and generate_slight_improvement.
- **rephrase**: For `instruction_type == 'default'`, the flow is style-only (no job description or key_requirements in the prompt). For grammar, shorten, formal, casual, expand, and custom, key_requirements is included and section prompts are job-aligned.
